Chest infection (pneumonia)

A bacterial infection in part of the lung. Most people recover fully with antibiotics, rest and fluids over 1-3 weeks; tiredness and cough can last longer.

Atrial fibrillation (irregular heartbeat)

Atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular response · likely (confirm on 12-lead ECG)

The heart's upper chambers beat chaotically. The two goals are to keep the heart rate comfortable and to prevent blood clots that can cause a stroke. A chest infection can trigger AF; it may settle as the infection clears.

High cholesterol / heart risk
